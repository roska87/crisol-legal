Privacy policy

Effective with Crisol version 1.3 (PUBLICATION_DATE); previous version: October 17, 2026. This is a translation; the Spanish version is the original.

In short: Crisol does not collect your data. What you enter is stored on your device and, if you have Premium with iCloud sync turned on, also in your private iCloud. The app has no servers of its own, uses no advertising or analytics tools, and shares no information with third parties.

Who is responsible

Crisol is an app developed by Ari Rostkier, in Uruguay. For any privacy question, write to us at crisol.app.soporte@gmail.com.

What data the app uses and where it stays

  • What you enter (accounts, balances, cards, transactions, installments, recurring items, merchants, products and your activities —job, company or rental— with their payments, deductions and taxes): stored on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, also in your private iCloud (see below). Nobody else, including us, has access to it.
  • Cards: the app never asks for or stores a full card number; at most, the last four digits you choose to enter to recognize it.
  • Preferences: device preferences (whether you hide amounts, the lock or which side the “+” button is on) are stored on that device and not synced. Those that belong to your data (the default account for each currency, the order of sections and the taxes you set up) travel with your data and, if you use iCloud, are synced too.

iCloud sync

It is part of Crisol Premium. When you get Premium it turns on by itself (the app tells you) and you can turn it off anytime in Settings › iCloud. It lets you see the same data on your devices with the same Apple Account.

  • Your data is stored in the private database of your iCloud account. Apple manages it under its iCloud terms: neither we nor anyone else has access to it.
  • If you turn it off or Premium expires, syncing stops: each device keeps its copy and what is in iCloud stays saved until you delete it.

Camera and photos

  • Scanning receipts: if you use the camera or choose a photo, the receipt text is read on your device. The image is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • “Visible while looking” (optional): if you turn it on, the front camera is used to detect whether there is a face looking at the screen and how many, in order to show or hide amounts; it also estimates the ambient light, to warn you when there is too little to see you. That analysis happens in the moment and on your device: no images or face data are stored, sent anywhere or used for any other purpose, including advertising or analytics.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, or when showing hidden amounts (an option you can turn off in Settings › Lock and privacy), the app asks iOS to confirm your identity. The app never receives or stores your biometric data: it only receives the answer from iOS (confirmed or not).

Notifications

Due-date reminders are scheduled on your device. You turn them on only if you want, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Suggestions with Apple Intelligence

If you use it, category suggestions for the products on a receipt are made with the Apple Intelligence model that runs on your device. It only receives the names of the products and of your categories.

Purchases and subscription

The Crisol Premium subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may share aggregated, anonymous sales information with us, under its own policies.

Tracking and third parties

Crisol does not track you across apps or websites, does not show advertising, does not use third-party analytics tools, and does not sell or share data.

How to delete your data

To delete everything from the app: Settings › Danger zone › Delete all data. If you use iCloud sync, it is also deleted from iCloud and your other devices.

You can also delete the app, which removes what is on that device. If you used iCloud sync, delete what is in iCloud from iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Crisol.

Children

Crisol is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data, we have no information about you to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question or request related to Uruguay’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 18,331, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
