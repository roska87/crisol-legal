Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026. This is a translation; the Spanish version is the original.

In short: Crisol does not collect your data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, the app has no servers of its own, uses no advertising or analytics tools, and shares no information with third parties.

Who is responsible

Crisol is an app developed by Ari Rostkier, in Uruguay. For any privacy question, write to us at crisol.app.soporte@gmail.com.

What data the app uses and where it stays

  • What you enter (accounts, balances, cards, transactions, installments, recurring items, merchants and products): stored only on your device. Nobody else, including us, has access to it.
  • Cards: the app never asks for or stores a full card number; at most, the last four digits you choose to enter to recognize it.
  • Preferences (for example, whether you hide amounts or which side the “+” button is on): stored on your device.

Camera and photos

  • Scanning receipts: if you use the camera or choose a photo, the receipt text is read on your device. The image is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • “Visible while looking” (optional): if you turn it on, the front camera is used to detect whether there is a face looking at the screen and how many, in order to show or hide amounts. That analysis happens in the moment and on your device: no images or face data are stored, sent anywhere or used for any other purpose, including advertising or analytics.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, the app asks iOS to confirm your identity. The app never receives or stores your biometric data: it only receives the answer from iOS (confirmed or not).

Notifications

Due-date reminders are scheduled on your device. You turn them on only if you want, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Suggestions with Apple Intelligence

If you use it, category suggestions for the products on a receipt are made with the Apple Intelligence model that runs on your device. It only receives the names of the products and of your categories.

Purchases and subscription

The Crisol Premium subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may share aggregated, anonymous sales information with us, under its own policies.

Tracking and third parties

Crisol does not track you across apps or websites, does not show advertising, does not use third-party analytics tools, and does not sell or share data.

How to delete your data

Since everything stays on your device, deleting the app is enough to delete your data. If iCloud sync is added in the future, your data will be in your iCloud account and you will be able to delete it from iOS Settings; this policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

Crisol is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data, we have no information about you to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question or request related to Uruguay’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 18,331, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
