Help and contact

A question, a bug or an idea? Write to us at crisol.app.soporte@gmail.com. If it is a bug, tell us what you were doing and, if you can, send a screenshot (hide the amounts with the eye if you want).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Crisol works without sign-up and your data is stored only on your device.

How do I get started?

Add your accounts with their actual balance and, if you have any, your cards with their closing and due days. Then record expenses, income and transfers with the “+” button.

How do I hide amounts?

Tap the eye at the top right. If you press and hold it, you can turn on “visible while looking”, which shows amounts only while you are looking at the screen (it needs a device with Face ID).

How do I restore Premium on another device?

Go to Settings › Premium and tap “Restore purchases”, with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel the subscription?

From Settings › Premium › “Manage subscription”, or in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If Premium expires, everything you recorded remains available.

Can I have the same data on my iPhone and iPad?

Not yet: for now, data stays on each device.
